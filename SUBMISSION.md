# Submission fields
**Project Title:** FasalProof: Satellite Proof for Every Farmer's Crop Insurance Claim

**Problem Statement:** Millions of Indian farmers lose crop insurance claims because they can't prove crop loss in time. FasalProof uses free Sentinel-2 satellite imagery to measure vegetation damage on a farmer's plot and generates a claim-ready evidence report in their own language, at zero cost.
