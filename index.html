    🔬 Methodology & honest limitations

    NDVI = (NIR−Red)/(NIR+Red) from Sentinel-2 (10 m). We compare the plot's peak pre-loss health with the average after the loss date, after masking clouds and shadows per pixel. NDWI flags standing water. Rainfall and temperature come from Open-Meteo reanalysis. Limits: NDVI cannot tell which crop is growing, small plots are approximated by a 200 m square, and results are supporting evidence, not an official crop-cutting assessment. Next step: integrate state land-record (Bhulekh) boundaries.
