Privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026. This is a plain-language description of how the app works, written by the FasalProof team. It is not legal advice.

Short version

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no advertising, and the app sets no cookies.
  • Your name, village and the text of your letter and report stay in your browser. They are never sent to our server.
  • To analyse a field, our server receives the map coordinates, the loss date and a few analysis settings.

What our server receives

When you press "Run satellite analysis" the app sends latitude, longitude, the loss date and the analysis window to our API (hosted on Render). It uses them to fetch public satellite images and returns the result. The server keeps results in memory for up to one hour to speed up repeat requests; it has no database. For rate limiting it also keeps recent request times per IP address in memory. Standard hosting logs (such as request time and IP address) may be kept by the hosting provider. When you sign a report, only a SHA-256 fingerprint of the report is sent, not the report.

What stays on your device

  • Browser storage: your recent analyses and the last result (so you can reload them), whether experimental features are switched on, and a queue of requests made while offline.
  • App files cache: the service worker keeps the app's files so it opens faster and works offline.
  • Use "Clear my data on this device" in the app's About panel to remove all of this, or clear your browser's site data.

Third-party services your browser contacts

  • Open-Meteo: place search, elevation and weather history (place names or coordinates).
  • BigDataCloud: reverse geocoding (coordinates) to show the place name and country.
  • Esri: satellite map tiles (the tile locations you view, plus your IP address).
  • Google Fonts: font files (your IP address).
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • jsDelivr and Hugging Face: only if you switch on experimental AI features, to download the AI library and models. The AI then runs on your device; your photos and voice are not uploaded.

Our server, on your behalf, also contacts Earth Search / AWS (Sentinel-2) and, for the experimental radar check, Microsoft Planetary Computer (Sentinel-1). These receive the plot's location and dates.

Sharing

The "Copy shareable link" button builds a link that contains the inputs you entered, including name and village, inside the link itself. Share it only with people you trust. Share buttons such as WhatsApp open that service with the text you choose; nothing is sent unless you press them.

Microphone, camera and location

The app asks for your location only if you press "My location". The microphone is used only for the experimental voice search, processed on your device. Photos used by the experimental photo check are processed on your device.

Analytics

No analytics are enabled by default. If the team turns on page-view counting, it will use a cookieless counter that records page visits without personal data, and this page will be updated.

Your choices and contact

You can use the tool without providing a name or village. To ask a question or request deletion of anything we might hold, contact us through GitHub Issues.
