What FasalProof is

FasalProof compares free satellite images and weather records to give supporting evidence about possible crop damage. It is not an official assessment. It does not decide whether a claim is valid or how much is paid. Your insurer, bank or the competent authority makes those decisions.

Accuracy and validation

Results are estimates with stated uncertainty. They can be wrong: cloud cover, crop ripening, small plots and data gaps all affect them. Validation against documented events is in progress and no accuracy figure is guaranteed. Insurance rules shown for India reflect published PMFBY guidelines at the time of writing and may change; rules for other countries come from the policy details you enter.

Do not abuse the service (for example automated high-volume requests; the API is rate-limited), attempt to disrupt it, or present generated evidence as an official document or alter a signed report. Check the information you submit to your insurer for accuracy.
